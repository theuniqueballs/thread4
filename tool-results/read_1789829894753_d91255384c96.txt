     1→PALETTE_LIBRARY.yaml — THREAD 3.2-EXP V2
     2→50 new palettes (P21-P70) for the 21×21 palette-rotation system.
     3→Each palette has 6 color slots (vs the 3-slot P01-P20 palettes):
     4→dominant, secondary, accent1, accent2, shadow, forbidden
     5→Plus sd_fragment (rendering hint) and mood (emotional register).
     6→Design philosophy: VARIETY over UNITY. The 3-slot palettes of P01-P20
     7→produced stylistically-uniform batches (one dominant hue repeated 7×).
     8→The 6-slot palettes force color DIVERSITY: every prompt can carry its own
     9→unique color harmony without repeating another prompt's accent choice.
    10→Usage with the VOID batch (21×21 mode): 21 palettes drawn from this
    11→library, each with a "void backdrop" structure — deep dominant as the
    12→void, accent1 as the body-light, accent2 as the setting-edge.
    13→C001_NEW (replaces C001): batch uses ≥7 distinct palettes by default.
    14→C001_EXP (VOID mode): 21 distinct palettes for 21 prompts.
    15→version: "1.0.0"

   732→═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
   733→DIAGNOSIS (scripts/color_audit.py, machine evidence, 2026-09-10):
   734→- N20: DESAT/SAT word ratio 1.66, 7/21 wash-risk prompts (worst of series)
   735→- N18: ~1.2, 6/21 · N19: 0.85, 5/21
   736→- The library's own vocabulary (pale-/faint-/smoke-/ash-/fog-/worn- words,
   737→  REQUIRED in POS by C069) + diffuse-dominant light types + ZERO anti-wash
   738→  NEG terms = the model renders a milky film over otherwise good palettes.
   739→  The palette is not the disease; the REGISTER is. Author's complaint,
   740→  verbatim: «Бывает, что сама палитра хороша, а на картинках будто маска
   741→  какой-то блеклости» — the named desaturated words ARE the mask.
   742→
   743→THE FIVE LOCKS (binding from N21; full law text in RULES §51):
   744→1. DEEP-ANCHOR LOCK: the quality closer anchors the dominant as DEPTH,
   745→   never as pallor — "deep-mist-grey depth", never "pale-fog tones"; every
   746→   pale-family word in the closer is paired with a depth-qualifier in the
   747→   same phrase.
   748→2. SATURATED ANCHOR LOCK (one per prompt): every palette names ONE
   749→   full-saturation element — accent1 when accent1 is saturated, else its
   750→   highest-energy slot (the vermilion stroke, the blood-edge, the coral
   751→   anomaly, the gilt thread). It appears in POS prose as a NAMED saturated
   752→   color doing visible work. Monochrome+1-saturated is the classic
   753→   punch-through; even P29_VOID_FOG carries its silver-edge at full polish.
   754→3. CONTRAST CARRIER LOCK: every POS carries >=1 light-contrast carrier
   755→   (rim, specular, wet-highlight, hard shadow line, caustic, corona) —
   756→   contrast reads as color; a washed frame is a flat frame. FR4 preferred
