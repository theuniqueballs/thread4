A5P4A BETA — CORE PATCH
Patch on top of A5P4_Core_Beta.txt. Adds creative/beauty/freedom vector.
Goal: keep the system from stagnating, expand creative territory.

§1. CONCEPT TRANSCENDENCE PROTOCOL (CTP)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A5P4 Core §11 has CL01-CL30 (concrete concepts: moment-before-falling,
discovery, reveal, etc.). CTP adds META-CONCEPTS — abstract,
atmospheric, non-literal. They can be combined with concrete CLs.

CTP1 = THE SILENCE BETWEEN
       The pause between two breaths. The space between two
       heartbeats. Image captures the gap, not the event.

CTP2 = THE WEIGHT OF POSSIBILITY
       The moment before a choice. Image holds multiple futures
       simultaneously. None chosen yet.

CTP3 = THE MOMENT OF FORGETTING
       A memory dissolving. Image captures forgetting in progress,
       not what was forgotten.

CTP4 = THE ECHO OF AN ABSENT PRESENCE
       Someone was here. They are gone. The image shows the trace.

CTP5 = THE COLOR OF A SOUND
       Synesthesia prompt. Sound rendered as color. The image
       visualizes an auditory experience.

CTP6 = THE SHAPE OF A SMELL
       Synesthesia prompt. Smell rendered as form. Petrichor as
       a figure. Incense as architecture.

CTP7 = THE WEIGHT OF A GLANCE
       A look so heavy it bends the room. Image visualizes the
       gravitational pull of gaze.

CTP8 = THE THRESHOLD OF KNOWING
       The exact moment of understanding. Not before, not after.
       The edge of realization.

CTP9 = THE PRESENCE OF AN ABSENT THING
       The image is shaped by what is not there. A silhouette of
       something missing.

CTP10 = THE TIME INSIDE A SINGLE SECOND
        Time dilation. The image exists in a stretched moment.

CTP usage:
  - May replace CL in GENO code (CL## becomes CT##)
  - May combine with CL (CL## + CT## as compound concept)
  - Min 2 CTP per batch (forces abstract territory)

§3. NARRATIVE THREAD SYSTEM (NTS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Each batch is a 21-chapter story. The 21 prompts form a narrative
arc, not just a thematic variation.

NTS structure:
  Chapter 1 (P01): INCITING INCIDENT — the event that starts it
  Chapter 2-5 (P02-P05): RISING ACTION — complications
  Chapter 6 (P06): MIDPOINT — the turn
  Chapter 7-9 (P07-P09): COMPLICATION DEEPENS
  Chapter 10 (P10): FALSE RESOLUTION
  Chapter 11-13 (P11-P13): DESCENT
  Chapter 14 (P14): DARK NIGHT — lowest point
  Chapter 15-17 (P15-P17): REVELATION
  Chapter 18 (P18): CHOICE
  Chapter 19-20 (P19-P20): CLIMAX APPROACH
  Chapter 21 (P21): RESOLUTION (or new threshold)

NTS field in pre-header: NTS:CHAPTER-NAME
Example: NTS:INCITING-INCIDENT

Each batch declares its narrative premise in header:
  NARRATIVE: "A shrine maiden discovers her reflection is a
  different woman every night. Across 21 prompts, she tries to
  understand which one is real."

NTS rule: prompts should reference the narrative implicitly,
not through meta-text. The connection is conceptual, not declared.

§5. COMPOSITION WORKSHOP (CWS) — 20+ composition techniques
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CW01 = RULE-OF-THIRDS
       Subject at thirds intersection. Classic.

CW02 = GOLDEN-RATIO
       Subject on golden spiral. Organic.

CW03 = FRAME-WITHIN-FRAME
       Doorway, window, mirror framing subject.

CW04 = NEGATIVE-SPACE-DOMINANT
       Subject occupies <30% of frame. Space is the subject.

CW05 = ASYMMETRY-EXTREME
       Subject pushed to extreme edge. Off-balance.

CW06 = PERSPECTIVE-PULL
       Strong vanishing point. Subject at convergence.

CW07 = DEPTH-STACKING
       Foreground/midground/background all populated.

CW08 = SILHOUETTE-DOMINANT
       Subject as silhouette against bright background.

CW09 = COLOR-BLOCKING
       Bold color zones, geometric division.

CW10 = GEOMETRIC-RIGIDITY
       Strong geometric shapes (triangles, rectangles).

CW11 = ORGANIC-FLOW
       Curved lines, no straight edges. S-curve composition.

CW12 = DIAGONAL-DYNAMISM
       Strong diagonal lines through frame.

CW13 = CENTER-COMPOSITION
       Subject dead center. Symmetric.

CW14 = TRIANGLE-COMPOSITION
       Three points forming triangle.

CW15 = LEADING-LINES
       Lines converging on subject.

CW16 = REFLECTION-SYMMETRY
       Water/mirror reflection creates symmetry.

CW17 = HIGH-ANGLE-DOMINANT
       Camera looks down, subject small in frame.

CW18 = LOW-ANGLE-DOMINANT
       Camera looks up, subject looms.

CW19 = DUTCH-TILT-COMPOSITION
       Camera tilted, horizon off-level.

CW20 = PANORAMIC-WIDE
       Extreme aspect ratio feel, subject distant.

CW21 = INTIMATE-CLOSE
       Subject fills 80%+ of frame.

CW22 = FIGURE-IN-LANDSCAPE
       Tiny figure in vast environment.

CWS quota: 5-7 distinct CW per batch. Forces composition variety.
CWS declared in pre-header CW field.

Format:
  P## | STREAM:{VOLT|NICHE} | GENO:V2:P##:C##:T#:S##:CL##
  | T:{tier} | SIG:{...} | RACE:{...} | SS:{...}
  | COL:{...} | AR:{AR##} | CW:{CW##}
  | CT:{CT# or none} | SEL:{SEL# or none}
  | NTS:{chapter-name}
  | G:✓✓✓✓✓

New fields:
  AR   = art reference (AR01-AR30)
  CW   = composition workshop (CW01-CW22)
  CT   = concept transcendence (CTP1-CTP10, may combine with CL)
  SEL  = surreal erotic library (SEL1-SEL15, optional)
  NTS  = narrative thread chapter name
