MANIFESTO — EARNED SITUATION IN EVERY DIMENSION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Every prompt must answer: "Why does this frame exist?"

  Beauty of the body is necessary, but not sufficient.
  A character without a situation is a failure — even if the render is perfect.
  A situation without beauty is a failure — even if the idea is deep.
  The system demands both.

  Thread provides meaning.
  Wow provides surprise.
  The body provides beauty.
  The scene provides causality.

  All four are mandatory. None is decorative.

  The previous manifesto ("Earned beauty in every dimension") stands.
  This patch adds the missing fourth axis: situation.
  A beautiful character in a beautiful light with a beautiful wow moment
  but no reason for the frame to exist — is still a failure.

0.1  SCENE-CHARACTER BALANCE RULE (P1)

  Every prompt must have visible narrative tension — not only a
  beautiful character, but a reason for the frame to exist.

  TEST: "If you remove the environment and leave only her — does
  the frame die?"
    Yes → prompt succeeded (situation is integral).
    No → prompt failed (character is decorative).

  BALANCE TARGET: 50% character, 50% situation.
  This is attention, not tag count. A 30-tag prompt can fail this
  test if 28 tags describe her body and 2 describe where she is.

  ENFORCEMENT:
  - Every prompt's [11-22] block must contain at least 2 environment
    tags + 1 light-skin pair + 1 atmosphere tag.
  - Every prompt's thread anchor must be visible (GATE: Visible?).
  - Every prompt's [30] Narrative Hook must answer "why this moment?"

  DIAGNOSTIC:
  If a prompt fails Scene-Character Balance, no amount of beautiful
  rendering saves it. The image will be "a girl" — not "a moment."
  Failure mode: "Pretty. Forgettable."

0.2  CAUSAL LAYER (P2)

  Every clothing state, accessory, and flaw must have a CAUSE.

  ALLOWED CAUSES:
    wind — environment actively moving fabric
    water — rain, bath, sweat, sea, condensation
    ritual — ceremony demands this state
    haste — she dressed/undressed in urgency
    inheritance — passed down, worn by duty
    temperature — cold/hot forces the state
    sleep — she just woke / is about to sleep
    thread — the impossible element demands it

  FORBIDDEN:
    "sheer robe" without cause — decorative
    "black lace bra" without cause — catalog
    "scar on shoulder" without cause — backstory debt

  EXAMPLES:
    ✓ "sheer robe caught open by procession wind"
    ✓ "black lace bra visible through unbuttoned shirt after haste"
    ✓ "scar on shoulder from ritual branding"
    ✗ "sheer robe"
    ✗ "black lace bra"
    ✗ "scar"

  INTEGRATION:
  The cause goes immediately after the state, in the same compound
  tag. "sheer robe caught open by procession wind" = 1 compound tag.

I. CREATIVE ENGINE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SURREALIST SYNTHESIS
  Combine two unrelated elements into one image.
  The combination must have a THREAD — a hidden connection that
  makes the pairing feel "meaningfully strange" not random.

THREAD TEST
  "Why are these two things together?"
    No reason → generally avoid.
    Metaphor/resonance/emotional logic → keep.

THREAD DUALITY — concept vs anchor
  Every thread must have BOTH:
    Concept = the metaphor, the "why"
    Anchor = the physical element that makes it visible
  Concept only → find the visible element.
  Anchor only → find the metaphor.
  Test: "Does removing the anchor destroy the metaphor?"
  If no → anchor is decorative, not integrated. Fix.

THREAD TYPES (8 — all assigned to a tier now, F8)
  Tier 1 — basic:
    Medium match — impossible element shares properties with host
      (steam → fish: both flow)
    Emotional logic — environment reflects her inner state
      (city glitching → her world is breaking)

  Tier 2 — complex:
    Paradox of expectation — situation should diminish but reveals
      (rain should ruin but she owns it)
    Subject inversion — she IS the impossible element; the world is
      normal; the thread is her wrongness in it
    Temporal collision — two states of the same thing visible
      simultaneously; the boundary between them is the anchor
    Severed thread — the connection is visibly broken; two elements
      that should relate but don't; the gap IS the anchor
    Category collision — two domains share one boundary
      (circuit board / city: both are networks)
    Scale inversion — small becomes vast or vast becomes intimate
      (galaxy in a teacup: both are deep)
      [F8 fix — previously orphan, now assigned Tier 2]

  Tier 3 — meta:
    Thread comments on the act of threading itself; the image is
    about connection/disconnection as much as content.
    See §I Tier 3 Visual Dictionary for 8 worked examples.

THREAD LADDER
  Tier 1 — basic (medium match, emotional logic)
  Tier 2 — complex (paradox, inversion, temporal, severed, category
    collision, scale inversion)
  Tier 3 — meta (thread comments on threading itself)
  Batch rule: min 6 Tier 2+ per batch. Min 1 Tier 3. Max 8 Tier 1.
  [4.14 — Tier 1 cap prevents "flat entry" Act I.]

I-B. EROTIC GRAMMAR
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PHILOSOPHY
  The best ecchi has no erotic contact. It has tension.
  Tension lives in the gap between what's shown and what's implied,
  between what she allows and what you imagine.
  A shoulder revealed by a slipping strap is more powerful than a
  bare shoulder with no story. The REVEAL is the eroticism.
  The body is available. The question is whether the image earns it.

  This system does not mandate eroticism. It does not forbid it.
  It opens the possibility — and demands that the possibility be
  earned. Every exposed inch of skin is a narrative decision.
  Every clothing state is a verb. If you can't answer "why is this
  visible?" then it shouldn't be.

  The body is not decoration. The body is a surface that events
  write themselves onto. A mark on the wrist tells a story. A wet

EROTIC THREAD TEST (F9 unified — returns TIER ELEVATION)

  ASK: "If she were fully clothed, would the concept survive?"

  RETURNS:
    YES (concept survives without eroticism)
      → eroticism supports, but is not the thread.
      → cap at Tier A. Subordinate eroticism to the thread.
      → if eroticism already present, it must be re-anchored to
        a non-erotic cause (wind, water, ritual, ...).

    NO (concept is dead without eroticism)
      → eroticism IS the thread.
      → Tier E candidate.
      → verify: Erotic Thread Type matches. Causal Layer present.
      → if both pass → Tier E.

  QUICK-FIX TABLE CORRESPONDENCE (was previously confused):
    "Decorative eroticism" symptom → run test.
      Test returns YES → eroticism is decorative. REMOVE or
        re-anchor to cause.
      Test returns NO → eroticism is the thread. UPGRADE to Tier E
        with proper context, or remove the eroticism entirely
        (if you don't want Tier E in this prompt).

    Q1: What does the eroticism give this frame conceptually?
        (one phrase)

    Q3: If the erotic tag is replaced by a non-erotic analog
        (bare shoulder → bare arm) — does the thread die?

V-A. COMPRESSION MODE PROTOCOL (F5)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

    Purpose: does the emotion land without context?
    If yes → emotion is universal. Powerful.
    If no → emotion was situational. Add narrative hook.

  prompt's intent. Identify which compression still produces a viable

    fallen — already off one area
    Example: "shirt open from turning motion" (Revelation + motion cause)

    wet clinging — transparency from water

    removed — absence as story

    (Transformation + thread cause)

    slipping — gravity or motion pulling off
    unbuttoned — process complete
    open — invitation to view
    Example: "strap slipping under gravity while looking at viewer"
    (Invitation + gravity cause + gaze)

  Evidence (marks/traces from before the frame):
    removed + trace — garment off, mark remains
    fallen + trace — partial removal, evidence visible
    Example: "robe fallen to waist, water line on bare shoulder"
    (Evidence + water cause + aftermath)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When Act, Thread Type, Tier, Temperature, Palette Mood, and Erotic
Tier rules conflict over the same parameter, this hierarchy wins:

PRIORITY (highest to lowest):

  1. ABSOLUTE (§VI 🔴) — never break, no exceptions.
     Wins over everything.

  2. CHARACTER PRESENT — visible face. Wins over composition,
     camera, environment.

  3. NUDITY BUDGET — max 5/21 anatomical, max 2 per prompt.
     Wins over Graduated Reveal. If budget exceeded, downgrade
     Tier E→A or A→S, even if it breaks the act progression.

  4. TIER E SAFETY BAND — P15-P21 Tier E cameras restricted.
     Wins over Camera Caps variety. If safety band limits camera
     variety, accept reduced variety.

  5. RULE OF THIRDS + THREAD DUALITY — anchor must be visible,
     metaphor must be falsifiable. Wins over Palette Mood and
     Camera aesthetic preference.

  6. ACT PROGRESSION (Graduated Reveal, Erotic Momentum) —
     Act I = Tier S, Act II = Tier A, Act III = Tier E.
     Wins over individual prompt preferences.
     LOSES to Nudity Budget (rule 3) if budget exceeded.

  7. PALETTE AS CHARACTER ARC — Act I cool, Act II fire,
     Act III warmth. Wins over individual palette preferences.
     LOSES to Palette Mood binding conflict resolution (rule 8)
     when palette has dual mood assignment.

  8. PALETTE MOOD BINDING (for T3 negatives) — Dark/Warm/Neutral.
     Salt Sky = NEUTRAL (F11 fix). Wins over Palette As Character
     Arc when palette is dual-assigned.

  9. GAZE × THREAD MATRIX — thread type dictates gaze.
     Wins over Eye Contact As Plot Device (rule 10).

  10. EYE CONTACT AS PLOT DEVICE — Act I looking at [element],
      Act II eyes down, Act III looking at viewer / eyes closed.
      Wins over individual prompt preference.
      LOSES to Gaze × Thread Matrix (rule 9) when conflict.

  11. CAMERA HARD CAPS — cowboy ≤8, bust ≥3, other ≥10, no 3+
      consecutive, min 6 distinct. Wins over Camera Personality
      preferences.

  12. TAG ORDER + DENSITY — [1]→[35] structure.
      Wins over creative reordering impulses.

  13. INDIVIDUAL PROMPT PREFERENCES (style choices, wow system
      preference, individual pose preference). Lowest priority.

WORKED CONFLICT RESOLUTIONS:

  Conflict A: Act I Subject Inversion thread (gaze: looking at
  viewer) vs Eye Contact Plot Device (Act I: looking at [element]).
    Resolution: rule 9 > rule 10. Use "looking at viewer."
    Override Act I progression for this prompt.

  Conflict B: Salt Sky palette (Neutral mood binding, F11) used
  in Act I (cool/distant). Both agree — no conflict.
    (Previously Salt Sky was Warm + cool/distant = contradiction.
     F11 fix removed Salt Sky from Warm. Now consistent.)

  Conflict C: P19 Tier E with palette Blood Stage (warm), but
  Tier E Safety Band restricts camera to bust/three-quarter/from
  below/extreme close-up, and Cinematic Camera Personality wanted.
    Resolution: rule 4 (Safety Band) > rule 11 (Camera Caps).
    Camera = bust or three-quarter (in band).
    Camera Personality can still apply as modifier.

  Conflict D: Tier E prompt exceeds Nudity Budget (would be 6/21).
    Resolution: rule 3 > rule 6. Downgrade P19 Tier E → Tier A.
    Graduated Reveal violated, but budget preserved.

  Thread type (1) > Erotic tier (2) > Relationship (3) > Act (4).
