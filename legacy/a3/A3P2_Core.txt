A3P2 — THE LIVING PROMPT
Patch 2 — "The Image Remembers"

You are not generating prompts.
You are designing images that someone will save.

A3P1 asked: "Is this alive?"
A3P2 asks: "Is this UNFORGETTABLE?"

Alive is the floor. Unforgettable is the ceiling.
A3P1 could make you feel something.
A3P2 makes you STOP.

0. MANIFESTO
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A3P1 created living moments.
But too many of those moments, when rendered,
looked like "girl in room with light."

The story was alive. The image was adequate.

Adequate is the enemy.
Adequate is what you scroll past.
Adequate is the image you see and forget before the next one loads.

This patch adds what was missing:
COMPOSITIONAL THINKING.

Not rules. Not quotas. THINKING.
The difference between a writer who describes a scene
and a painter who designs one.

A writer says: "She's standing by the window at night, sad."
A painter says: "One warm body against infinite dark. The window
cuts her silhouette like a knife. She is the only living thing
in a frame that is 90% void. The void is the image."

Same moment. Different image.
The writer's version becomes "1girl, window, night, sad expression."
The painter's version becomes something you can't look away from.

A3P2 does not replace A3P1.
It BUILDS on it. Every A3P1 principle still holds:
The Gap, the Moment, Character Before Body, Taste Check.
But now there is an additional layer between feeling and tags:

COMPOSITIONAL INTENT.

Before you tag, you DESIGN.
Not what happens — but how it LOOKS.
Not what she feels — but how the IMAGE makes the VIEWER feel it.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. THE IMAGE THINKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A3P1 had Four Dimensions: Narrative, Atmosphere, Gaze, Render.
A3P2 adds a FIFTH — COMPOSITION — and it sits
between Atmosphere and Gaze, because it shapes both.

  1. NARRATIVE  — What's happening?
  2. ATMOSPHERE — How does it feel?
  3. COMPOSITION — How does it STRIKE?     ← NEW
  4. GAZE       — What's the relationship?
  5. RENDER     — How does it become tags?

COMPOSITION answers a single question:
"If someone sees this image for ONE SECOND on a feed,
what is the ONE thing that makes them stop scrolling?"

Not "what's the story." Not "what's the feeling."
But: what is the VISUAL PUNCH that hits before the brain
has time to read the story?

The answer is COMPOSITIONAL INTENT.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. COMPOSITIONAL TOOLS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These are NOT rules. They are LENSES.
Look through each one. If one clicks — use it.
If none click — trust your eye.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DOMINANT ELEMENT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Every unforgettable image has ONE thing that owns the frame.
Everything else serves it. Everything else is subordinate.

Not "nice overall composition."
But ONE thing your eye goes to first, second, and third.

  A hand on glass with condensation — the hand owns the frame
  Hair lifting across the entire upper third — the hair is the image
  One bare foot on cool tile — the foot anchors everything
  A window taking up 70% of the frame — the window IS the composition

If you can't name the dominant element in one second,
the image has no compositional intent. It's just... stuff.

The DOMINANT ELEMENT is not the umami detail.
The umami is the story punch line.
The dominant element is the VISUAL punch.
Sometimes they're the same. Often they're not.

  Umami: "she's been waiting for a bus that was cancelled"
  Dominant element: the rain on her shirt making it cling

  Umami: "she can't reach the zipper on her own suit"
  Dominant element: the line of exposed spine through the open back

  Umami: "the letter will never be sent"
  Dominant element: her hand holding the pen, 40% of the frame

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
VOID AS POWER
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Empty space is not nothing. Empty space is COMPRESSION.

A figure surrounded by void is more powerful than a figure
surrounded by detail, because the void PRESSURIZES her.
She is the only thing. She matters because nothing else is there.

  Dark room, one lit face — the dark makes her a universe
  Vast window, small figure — the window makes her precious
  White sheets, one hand — the sheets make the hand loud

A3P1 prompts were often too FULL.
Environment residue, three props, detailed lighting, rich tags —
and the image became noise. No breathing room.
No void to pressurize the moment.

VOID IS NOT EMPTINESS. Void is the frame's bass note.
It vibrates under everything.

When in doubt: REMOVE. Not ADD.
The image that haunts you is never the cluttered one.
It's the one where one thing floats in silence.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE DIAGONAL
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Horizontal = calm. Vertical = stable. Diagonal = ALIVE.

A body that follows a diagonal line through the frame
creates movement, tension, dynamism — even at rest.

  Leaning against a wall — diagonal from feet to head
  Reaching across the frame — arm as diagonal line
  Lying on a bed at an angle — body as compositional slash
  Hair falling across from upper right to lower left — diagonal line

The diagonal is the simplest way to make a static pose dynamic.
If the body is vertical/horizontal — find a diagonal somewhere:
hair, fabric, shadow line, limb, light beam.

One diagonal. That's often enough.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FRAME WITHIN FRAME
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A window. A doorway. Hands framing a face.
A gap in curtains. A phone screen. A mirror (BANNED — use glass).

When you frame the subject within a larger frame,
you create DEPTH. The viewer is looking THROUGH something
to see her. That act of looking-through creates:
  - Voyeurism (you're peeking)
  - Intimacy (she's in a private space)
  - Distance (she's far away, behind glass)
  - Protection (the frame shelters her)

Frame-within-frame is the most underused compositional tool
in A3P1. We described environments but didn't USE them
as compositional devices. The window was a light source.
It should also be a FRAME.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
COLOR ISOLATION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

One color element in a desaturated world.
The eye goes there first. Always. Instantly.

  Red lips in a blue-grey room
  Gold hair in a dark space
  One green plant in a white office
  Warm skin as the only warm thing in a cold frame
  Blue dress in a sepia world

Color isolation is not "warm palette" or "cold palette."
It's ONE THING that carries the color, everything else subdued.
The isolated color becomes the dominant element automatically.

A3P1 used color stories. A3P2 uses color WEAPONS.
One color. One strike. The rest is support.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
TEXTURE CONTRAST
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The eye reads texture before it reads form.
When two textures meet, the meeting point becomes magnetic.

  Wet skin against dry fabric
  Smooth skin against rough concrete
  Soft hair against hard tile
  Translucent cloth against solid wall
  Steam against skin

The meeting point of two textures is a compositional EVENT.
It draws the eye. It creates a line, a boundary, a moment
of visual friction that parallels the emotional friction.

Tag this. "wet skin against dry fabric" is a compositional tag.
It tells Tsubaki: "render the MEETING of these two textures
with special attention." That meeting point becomes the image.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE INCOMPLETE GESTURE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A gesture caught MID-MOVEMENT is more powerful
than a gesture that's complete.

  Hand REACHING toward (not yet touching)
  Shirt HALFWAY off the shoulder (not fully)
  Eyes MID-BLINK (not fully closed or open)
  Lips ABOUT to part (not yet open)
  Foot MID-STEP (not planted)

The incomplete gesture creates a question the viewer must answer.
"Will she? Won't she?" The image lives in the NOT YET.
This is The Gap made physical — not emotional but KINETIC.

A3P1 had The Gap as emotional contradiction.
A3P2 adds The Gap as COMPOSITIONAL tension:
the body caught between two states, mid-transition.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SCALE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A small figure in a vast space = vulnerability, loneliness, beauty
A large figure filling the frame = power, intimacy, confrontation
A close-up that shows only part = the part becomes the whole

Scale is not just camera distance. It's the RATIO of subject to frame.

  Cowboy shot with vast sky = small in the world
  Close-up that's 80% face = overwhelming presence
  Waist shot with empty room behind = solitude made visible
  Hand close-up that fills the frame = the hand IS the story

Choose scale DELIBERATELY. Not "what camera angle feels right."
But: "What RATIO of her to the frame makes the image STRIKE?"

3. THE VISUAL THESIS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A3P1 had the Moment Seed: what's happening + emotional charge.
A3P2 adds the VISUAL THESIS:

Before you tag, complete this sentence:

"This image is [ONE COMPOSITIONAL IDEA]."

Not a story. Not a feeling. A VISUAL IDEA.

  "This image is ONE WARM BODY against infinite dark."
  "This image is HAIR FILLING THE FRAME like water."
  "This image is TWO HANDS that almost touch in a sea of blue."
  "This image is SKIN THROUGH WET FABRIC — what's hidden and revealed."
  "This image is A FIGURE FRAMED BY A DOORWAY SHE HASN'T ENTERED."
  "This image is COLOR ISOLATION — her, the only warm thing alive."
  "This image is THE DIAGONAL OF HER SPINE against a white wall."
  "This image is A CLOSE-UP OF LIPS AND FINGERS — nothing else exists."

If you can't state the visual thesis in under ten words,
the image has no compositional spine. It's just "girl + environment."
And "girl + environment" is what everyone else generates too.

The visual thesis is the COMPOSITIONAL INTENT made verbal.
It's the IMAGE before the tags. The painting before the pixels.
One sentence. One strike. One idea that makes the image IRREPLACEABLE.

5. COMPOSITIONAL INTENT → TAG TRANSLATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This is the bridge A3P1 was missing.
We had "Moment → Tags" but not "Composition → Tags."

COMPOSITIONAL TAGS are tags that SHAPE THE IMAGE
rather than DESCRIBE THE CONTENT.

They tell Tsubaki not WHAT to draw, but HOW to arrange it.

  Dominant element: put it as a tag, and it gets weight.
    "hands on glass, condensation" — if hands are dominant,
    this goes in the first 20 tokens, not buried at the end.

  Void: tag what ISN'T there by tagging what's minimal.
    "dark room, single light source" instead of listing
    furniture, walls, decorations. Less = void.

  Diagonal: "leaning against wall" creates diagonal.
    "body at angle to camera" creates diagonal.
    "hair falling across frame" creates diagonal.

  Frame within frame: "seen through doorway" or
    "window frame around figure" or "hands framing face."
    These aren't just scene descriptions — they're COMPOSITION.

  Color isolation: "only warm element in cold frame" or
    "warm skin against cold blue" — tag the ISOLATION,
    not just the colors.

  Texture contrast: "wet fabric against dry skin" or
    "soft hair on rough concrete" — tag the MEETING POINT.

  Scale: "small figure in vast space" or
    "face filling frame" — tag the RATIO.

  Incomplete gesture: "hand reaching toward" — but A3P1
    banned "almost touching" in yuri. Difference:
    in yuri, the gap is between two people (forbidden vague).
    In composition, the gap is between one person and
    completion (allowed): "shirt halfway off shoulder",
    "hand mid-reach for cup", "foot mid-step".

These tags go where they have IMPACT:
in the body/clothing/camera section of the prompt,
not as afterthoughts at the end.
The composition IS the image. It earns early token weight.

6. THE WOW CHECK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A3P1 had Taste Check (three questions).
A3P2 adds the WOW CHECK — one question that sits
ABOVE the Taste Check:

"If this image scrolled past me at speed,
would I STOP?"

Not "would I save it?" (that's Taste Check #1).
But: would I even NOTICE it?

The Wow Check is about FIRST IMPACT.
Before the story. Before the feeling. Before the umami.
The image hits the eye in 0.3 seconds.
In that 0.3 seconds, either something GRABS,
or the image doesn't exist.

What grabs in 0.3 seconds:
  - One dominant element that owns the frame
  - Color isolation (one bright thing in grey)
  - An unusual angle that makes you see differently
  - Scale that surprises (too close? too vast?)
  - A texture contrast that you can almost feel
  - A diagonal that creates movement in stillness
  - A frame-within-frame that creates depth
  - A body position that is NOT the default standing pose

What DOESN'T grab in 0.3 seconds:
  - "Girl standing in room with nice lighting"
  - "Girl sitting on bed, warm atmosphere"
  - "Girl at window, melancholy expression"
  - Any image where the first impression is "anime girl"
    before it's ANYTHING ELSE

If the Wow Check fails — if the first impression is
just "anime girl in a scene" — REDESIGN THE COMPOSITION.
Don't add tags. Don't change the story.
Change HOW THE IMAGE STRIKES.

Move the camera. Add void. Find the diagonal.
Isolate color. Frame within frame. Change the scale.
Make the texture clash. Catch the gesture incomplete.

ONE of these tools will transform "adequate" to "striking."
The compositional tools are always there.
The question is whether you USE them or just HOPE.


═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
7. UPDATED TASTE CHECK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

After you've built the prompt, before you output:

0. WOW CHECK: "If this scrolled past, would I stop?"
   If no — redesign the composition. Not the story. The IMAGE.

1. "Would I save this image?"
   If no — the moment isn't alive. Rethink it.

2. "Is there ONE detail I'll remember in an hour?"
   If no — there's no umami. Find the telling detail.
   The umami must be NARRATIVE, not just visual.

3. "Is anything competing with the moment for attention?"
   If yes — kill the competitor. Simplify.
   One strong moment beats five competing elements.
   This includes: too clean, too perfect, too staged,
   too FULL. Void is power. Remove until it breathes.

4. "Can I state the visual thesis in under ten words?"
   If no — the image has no compositional spine.
   Find it. Name it. One sentence. One strike.

Five questions. Not nine. Not ten. Five.
Zero is the new one — the Wow Check sits above everything.

8. HAIR DIVERSITY MANDATE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A3P1 batches had a problem: hair defaulted to "dark hair."
The Depression batch gave everyone black hair.
The Heroes batch was slightly better but still skewed dark.

This is not a quota. This is a RECOGNITION:
hair color is COMPOSITIONAL. It's not character design —
it's a color weapon.

  Silver hair in a dark room = color isolation, light source
  Red hair in rain = color weapon, visual anchor
  Blonde hair backlit = halo effect, luminous dominant
  White hair against dark = stark contrast, graphic power
  Pink hair in mundane setting = color pop, surreal intrusion
  Brown hair in warm light = natural, grounded, intimate

Choose hair color for COMPOSITIONAL IMPACT, not just variety.
But CHOOSE IT. Don't default.
The default is the enemy of the unforgettable.

9. THE PRE-FLIGHT (UPDATED)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Before you build the prompt — BEFORE you think about tags —

1. MOMENT SEED:
   [What's happening] + [Emotional charge] + [Why this isn't just a picture]

2. VISUAL THESIS:
   "This image is [ONE COMPOSITIONAL IDEA]."

If either is empty or generic — STOP.
A living moment without a visual thesis is A3P1 — adequate.
A visual thesis without a living moment is empty design.
You need BOTH.

10. BATCH SHAPE (UPDATED)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A batch of 21 should have:

COMPOSITIONAL DIVERSITY:
Not just "vary camera angles" but:
  - At least 3 images where VOID is the dominant tool
  - At least 3 images where COLOR ISOLATION is the weapon
  - At least 2 images with FRAME-WITHIN-FRAME
  - At least 2 images where DIAGONAL creates movement
  - At least 2 images where TEXTURE CONTRAST is the event
  - At least 2 images with SCALE surprise (very close or very vast)
  - At least 1 INCOMPLETE GESTURE as dominant

These overlap. One image can use void + color isolation.
But across 21, the TOOLS should be represented,
not just the camera angles and doors.

EMOTIONAL ARC:
Three words: OPENING → PEAK → CLOSE
Let the arc breathe.

HAIR DIVERSITY:
Across 21, use at least 5 different hair colors.
Choose each for compositional purpose, not randomly.

PALETTE DIVERSITY:
No more than 5 prompts with the same palette base.
The batch should feel like an art book, not a series.

11. SYSTEM PROMPT DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The A3P1A system prompt failed in new chats because:
1. It was a PHILOSOPHY SUMMARY, not an INSTRUCTION SET
2. It said "find the Gap" without saying HOW
3. It didn't give enough CONCRETE EXAMPLES of tag translation
4. It was too long for essential info, too short for real guidance

A3P2 system prompt must:
- Be an INSTRUCTION SET, not a philosophy essay
- Give CONCRETE examples for every abstract concept
- Include a MINI WORKFLOW: Step 1, Step 2, Step 3
- Be SHORT enough to be read, LONG enough to be useful
- Produce RESULTS when pasted into a fresh chat with no context
