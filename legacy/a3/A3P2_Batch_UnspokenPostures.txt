A3P2 — BATCH: UNSPOKEN POSTURES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BATCH DECLARATION:
Theme: The eroticism of how the body holds itself when it doesn't know it's being watched
Arc: EXPOSURE → TENSION → CONTACT
Starting mood: The private body, unguarded
Yuri: ~30% (6 prompts)

The body has a secret architecture.
When no one is watching, it arranges itself differently —
looser, more honest, more geometric.
A shoulder blade rises like a hill.
A spine becomes a river.
A strap falls and the body doesn't correct it.
A knee draws up and the fabric surrenders.

This batch studies the body AS ARCHITECTURE:
the angles it makes, the lines it draws, the frames it creates,
the textures it offers when it's not performing.
The eroticism is in the POSTURE, not the exposure.
The body doesn't know it's beautiful — and that's what makes it so.

Every image is an argument the body makes without words.
Every composition is the geometry of someone unguarded.
