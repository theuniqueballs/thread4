# THREAD 2.0 — STYLE GUIDE
## Prose = instruction for Tsubaki 2 DiT, structured per PixAI block order.

## PIXAI BLOCK ORDER (MANDATORY)

PixAI recommends: **Composition → Lighting → Depth of Field → Angle → Subject → Environment → Details**

Style/composition MUST go at the BEGINNING, not as trailing tag.
"anime artstyle" as last token is treated as genre label, not rendering instruction.

## ATOMS (Prompt Structure — v2.0, PixAI-aligned)

Every prompt assembled in this order:
```
[COMPOSITION] → [LIGHTING] → [DOF] → [ANGLE] → [SUBJECT] → [ENVIRONMENT] → [DETAILS]
```

### COMPOSITION-ATOM (FIRST — sets tone for entire caption)
Style declaration + shot type + framing:
- "A 2D hand-drawn anime illustration, medium shot, cowboy framing."
- "A 2D hand-drawn anime illustration, wide environmental view, full body."
- "A 2D hand-drawn anime illustration, extreme close-up, macro detail."

**PH DEFENSE:** Style declaration at beginning MUST include "2D hand-drawn anime" — PH keeps
opening framing tone. Face geometry anchor in Subject block MUST include structural markers
("large expressive eyes, small nose, small mouth, flat cel-shaded skin") — PH may paraphrase
but keeps the semantic content if it's a complete sentence with specific anatomical terms.
DO NOT replace structural markers with vague phrases like "high-quality 2D anime style" —
PH will strip them.

### LIGHTING-ATOM (second — defines light before body)
LQ + LD + FR + CT integrated:
- "Soft diffused moonlight from off-frame, front and high, 2:1 fill, cool temperature."
- "Hard key light from off-frame, side and eye-level, 4:1 fill, warm on cheekbones, cool in shadowed hollows."

### DOF-ATOM (third — focus/blur)
What is sharp, what dissolves:
- "Sharp focus on her lips, the rest dissolving into soft bokeh."
- "Everything in focus, crisp detail from foreground to background."
- "Shallow depth of field, only her collarbones sharp, her face and body soft."

### ANGLE-ATOM (fourth — viewpoint, NO "camera" word!)
- "Viewed from above, looking down at her."
- "Viewed from below, looking up at her arched throat."
- "Viewed from the side, her profile silhouetted."
- "Viewed from over her shoulder, her back to the viewer."

### SUBJECT-ATOM (fifth — character, the longest block)
Order within subject: identity → hair → eyes → skin → face geometry → body → pose → wardrobe

**Face geometry anchor (MANDATORY, separate sentence with period):**
"Her face is rendered in stylized 2D anime style: large expressive eyes, small nose, small mouth, flat cel-shaded skin."

Body described through LIGHT INTERACTION (BPT rotation, ≤3 each per batch):
- BPT-1 (bust-first): "Her breasts catch the hard key as deep shadow pools beneath"
- BPT-2 (hips-first): "Her hips divide the rim-light into curved shadow"
- BPT-3 (thighs-first): "Her thighs hold shadow where diffused light cannot reach"
- BPT-4 (waist-first): "Her waist narrows where light converges and shadow deepens"
- BPT-5 (back-first): "Her back is canvas for the candle-glow, spine a river of warm shadow"
- BPT-6 (hands-first): "Her hands interrupt the key light, casting striped shadows across her collarbone"
- BPT-7 (silhouette-first): "Her silhouette against the window reads as abstract form before body resolves"
- BPT-8 (motion-first): "Her body mid-arch catches the beam while her stomach escapes into shadow"

### ENVIRONMENT-ATOM (sixth — setting + scene action)
Not "a room". Environment = source of reflected light + narrative context:
- "On a mirror-polished floor, flower petals suspended in the air around her."
- "In a bathroom filled with violet steam, the vapor concealing and revealing."

### DETAILS-ATOM (LAST — quality tags)
Palette-specific quality tags + "Masterpiece, best quality, anime artstyle."
- P01: "Noir velvet palette, high contrast, chiaroscuro. Masterpiece, best quality, anime artstyle."
- P07: "Frost lavender palette, silver and lavender, moonlit, ethereal. Masterpiece, best quality, anime artstyle."
