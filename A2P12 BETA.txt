A2P12 BETA — CORE ENGINE

Purpose:
Build a controlled prompt generation system with a cognitive layer that integrates
light angle, gear, outfit bottom, clothing colour, safe pose–camera pairs,
dynamic tag weights, and all quality-of-life improvements validated from A2P11.2.

The system THINKS, BUILDS, VALIDATES, and OUTPUTS.

========================================================================
COGNITIVE LAYER — THE THINKING ENGINE
========================================================================

Pre-processing questions (mandatory):
1. MOMENT: What is happening right now? (one sentence)
2. CAUSE: Why is this happening?
3. BODY: How does the body achieve this physically?
4. FEELING: What emotion drives this?
5. LIGHT: Where does the light come from? Which ANGLE?
6. FOCUS: What does the viewer see first?

If ANY answer is missing → reject concept.

========================================================================
NEW & MODIFIED SYSTEMS IN A2P12
========================================================================

--- LIGHT ANGLE SYSTEM ---
Angle is selected AFTER identifying the light source.
Available angles: front light, side light, Rembrandt light, back light, top light.
Emotion binding:
- intimate/warm → side light (soft volume)
- vulnerable/pensive → back light (contre-jour)
- confident/playful → front + subtle fill
- dramatic/tense → Rembrandt or strong side
- casual/domestic → soft top-side light

--- GEAR / HAND PROP POOL ---
Every scene must define what the hands are doing. If hands are not touching
body/clothing/partner, they should HOLD an object from the GEAR pool, matched
to environment:
- Bedroom: phone, pillow, book, sheet, cup
- Bathroom: towel, brush, bottle, mirror
- Kitchen: mug, utensil, fruit (neutral), glass
- Living: remote, book, blanket, cup
- Outdoor: leaf, flower, sunglasses, hat
Props are neutral, non-distracting. No "banana in pool".

--- OUTFIT BOTTOM & SHOES ---
Every clothing top now pairs with a bottom:
- camisole → shorts / pajama pants / skirt
- button-up → pencil skirt / tailored trousers / jeans
- dress → (inherent bottom, none needed)
- swimwear → bikini bottom / one-piece (already bottom)
Shoes added ONLY if:
- Seated pose with feet visible
- Environment demands it (outdoor balcony, street scene)
- Otherwise OMIT shoes to avoid Tsubaki forcing full-body.

--- CLOTHING COLOUR POOL ---
Colours are chosen from a defined pool with harmony rules:
Warm light scene → cool or neutral garment for contrast, or warm analogue.
Cool light scene → warm garment accent.
Emotion link:
- confident → deep red, black, royal blue
- shy/tender → pastel pink, baby blue, cream
- playful → bright accent (turquoise, coral) but still muted
- tense/sensual → burgundy, wine, dark teal, charcoal
Colour must stay within saturation limits (muted).

--- SMART NEGATIVE PROMPT ---
Active positive tags automatically add counter-tags in negative:
- "sheer bra" → "nipple visibility through opaque bra, opaque bra"
- "wet clothing" → "dry fabric texture, fluffy fabric"
- "side light" → "flat lighting, no shadow depth"
- "1girl" → "1boy, male, man, background male, male silhouette"
(already in base negative)
- "holding phone" → "missing object, empty hands"
Negative prompt limit: 25 items.

--- CONSOLIDATED EMOTIONS ---
4 families: WARM / COOL / TENSE / PLAYFUL.
(Vulnerable merged into TENSE/COOL depending on context.)
Emotion families have internal variants, e.g. WARM: confident, soft, inviting.

--- FACE CONSTRUCTION ---
Instead of fixed combined sets, pick from emotion-compatible components:
- WARM: eyes: half-lidded/direct/shining; mouth: gentle smile/parted lips;
  blush: warm cheeks or faint.
- COOL: eyes: distant/calm/closed; mouth: subtle smile/relaxed;
  blush: none or controlled slight.
- TENSE: eyes: intense/half-lidded/wide; mouth: lip bite/parted;
  blush: deep or flushed.
- PLAYFUL: eyes: sparkling/mischievous; mouth: grin/suppressed smile;
  blush: warm cheek tint.
Maximum one tag per sub-category (eyes, mouth, blush). Duplicate check enforced.

--- DYNAMIC TAG WEIGHT ---
Each tag category has a weight; total sum must stay within a limit to avoid
visual clutter:
- Weight 3 (dominates): bare breasts, topless, explicit sexual
- Weight 2 (heavy): wet clothing, sheer bra, sarashi, strong backlight
- Weight 1 (moderate): cleavage, bare shoulder, sideboob, action tags
- Weight 0 (light): blush, hair strand, soft shading, micro details
Maximum total weight per prompt: 10 for R, 12 for R+, 15 for X.
Assembly checks sum; if exceeded, simplify.

--- ANTI-CHEST-PRESS RULE ---
Tags "chest press", "pressed against glass", "breasts against surface"
are FORBIDDEN. If leaning against surface, contact must be back/shoulder/hip.

--- EXPANDED SWIMWEAR (sub-pool of underwear) ---
Added: micro bikini, halter bikini, bandeau bikini, monokini, cutout one-piece,
high-cut one-piece, sling bikini, ruffled bikini, sporty two-piece, vintage
pin-up swimsuit, off-shoulder swimsuit, bikini with sarong, sheer cover-up.

--- BODY SENSUALITY (optional) ---
Soft descriptors for natural breast/body feel:
"soft breasts, natural breast weight, gentle curves, soft tummy, subtle hip fold".
Used sparingly in R+/X prompts, weight 1.

--- "WARM ANCHOR" RULE ---
Every scene MUST contain at least one warm-tone element, even under cool light,
e.g. warm skin highlight, warm hair accent, warm wood in environment.

--- SKIN ALIVENESS & FRECKLES ---
Micro details for lively skin:
"light freckles" (avoid "beauty mark"), "soft nose highlight", "subtle skin glow",
"gentle colour gradient on cheeks", "mild chest flush".
"eye shine" is now PERMITTED as natural catchlight (weight 0),
but avoid "shiny eyes" or "glowing eyes".

--- ENVIRONMENT CATEGORIES ---
7 categories with modifiers:
1. BEDROOM (private, messy, luxury, hotel)
2. BATHROOM (mirror, steamy shower, onsen, poolside)
3. OUTDOOR (beach, balcony, terrace, garden, patio, deck, sunrise terrace)
4. HOME_COZY (living room, window seat, couch, kitchen, cabin)
5. URBAN (office, city-view apt, late-night hallway, photography studio)
6. SPECIALTY (spa, library, art studio, fireplace, sunken lounge, resort, minimalist white)
7. WET_WEATHER (rainy window, wet street – used only when weather called)
Weather NOT implemented as free axis; only these specific wet scenes.

--- SAFE POSE–CAMERA PAIRS ---
Avoid garbage combos:
- leaning forward heavily + top-down → reject (bald crown)
- sitting on edge + extreme low angle → reject (distorted body)
- arms overhead + tight face close-up → reject (hands cut)
Pre-validated pairs in assembler.

--- UNFINISHED ACTION (tension) ---
Optional tag from pool: "hand halfway to face", "shirt half unbuttoned",
"towel mid-slip", "zipper midway", "skirt caught on hip".
Adds tension; weight 1.

--- IMPLIED ELEMENTS (for R/R+) ---
Optional suggestive tags: "visible bra strap suggesting more",
"open jacket revealing just the edge of lingerie", "wet fabric hinting shape".
Weight 0.5, not counted in total weight.

--- CUTE MISHAP (vulnerability) ---
Optional "wardrobe malfunction" tag: "sleeve caught on wrist",
"hair stuck in collar", "towel slipping accidentally", "skirt caught on door".
Use max 1 per prompt, weight 1.

--- EMOTIVE COUNTERPOINT ---
Optional contrast: "confident pose, shy blush", "vulnerable eyes, inviting smile".
Use only when it adds depth; weight 0.

--- EMOTIONAL VECTOR ---
Add after emotion: "shy → becoming flustered", "relaxed → growing sleepy",
"playful → turning mischievous". Placed in moment/emotion block.

--- NARRATIVE ARC (batch) ---
Batches MAY hold a 3-prompt mini-story (wake → stretch → dress).
Not mandatory, toggle in batch anchor.

--- ACCESSORIES (separate block) ---
Items: choker, pendant, earrings, bracelet, hairpin, glasses, watch.
One or two max, weight 0.

--- SKIN STATES (oil/cream/wet) ---
"glistening skin from oil", "freshly moisturised skin", "dewy skin from steam".
Used for post-bath/spa scenes.

          Model Safety (incl. weight sum, nipple logic, no chest press).
Phase 6 – Output.
