  1.  MODEL AWARENESS — Tsubaki 2 uses Multi-Modal DiT 2.6A,
      NOT the same architecture as v1. Key differences:
      - Transformer attention processes all tokens in parallel
        (tag order matters less than with SD, but still matters
        for Prompt Helper rewrites)
      - Natural language + tags mixed style works BETTER than
        pure Danbooru tags
      - Prompt Helper v2 auto-rewrites; our system produces the
        INPUT that gets enhanced, not the final render instruction
      - 4 generation modes: Lite/Standard/Pro/Ultra — our prompts
        should work at Standard minimum, shine at Pro/Ultra
      - 25+ one-click styles available — our prompts should be
        style-agnostic (work with or without style overlay)
      - LoRA incompatible — we cannot rely on LoRA corrections

  2.  TAG ORDER UPDATE — [2] now supports hair color + length
      as a single compound tag. Tsubaki 2 handles compound
      descriptors better than v1. "long black hair" as one tag
      is fine. Don't split into "black hair, long hair" — that
      wastes a slot. Use the freed slot for something productive.

  SURFACE TRACE REVISION:

  3.  SURFACE TRACE DOWNGRADE — handprint/fingerprint-type traces
      removed from standard condiment rotation. Max 1 per batch.
      Reason: they render as strange artifacts, not as intended.
      The idea was better than the execution. Other trace types
      (water line, salt crust, ice trace, dew, condensation)
      remain — they render well because they're environmental,
      not body-on-body.

  WOW SYSTEMS (12 new techniques):

  4.  COLOR BREEDING — palette elements physically mix on surfaces.
      Red light + wet skin = blood-warm highlights. Blue shadow
      + blonde hair = green-tinted strands. The mixing IS the
      "wow." Tags: "red light on wet skin", "blue shadow tinting
      blonde hair green", "orange reflecting off bare shoulder"
      Tsubaki 2's dynamic light diffusion makes this sing.

  5.  POSE CONTRADICTION — body says one thing, face says another.
      Arms crossed (closed) + looking at viewer (open). Body
      turned away + face toward camera (refusal to leave).
      The contradiction IS the image. Deploy when emotional
      complexity needed. Not every prompt — 2-3 per batch.

  6.  WEIGHT SHIFT — character's weight visibly not centered.
      Leaning on one hip, mid-step, weight on railing, caught
      between positions. Makes the image feel like a frame from
      animation, not a posed photo. Tsubaki 2 handles natural
      movement well — use it.

  7.  TEXTURE INFECTION — one texture "infects" an adjacent surface.
      Rain on fabric makes fabric look like water. Candlelight
      on skin makes skin look like wax. Fog on hair makes hair
      look like smoke. The boundary between textures dissolves.
      Tags: "fabric looking wet as water", "skin golden like
      candlelight", "hair dissolving into fog"

  8.  NEGATIVE SPACE THEFT — the void steals color from the subject.
      Her hair color bleeds into the empty space behind her.
      Her silhouette leaves a color afterimage in the void.
      The emptiness isn't empty — it's where she was.
      Tags: "hair color bleeding into void", "color afterimage
      in negative space", "silhouette leaving warm trace"

  9.  ENVIRONMENTAL FRAMING — the world frames her without frames.
      Tree branches forming an arch. Doorway she's not walking
      through. Window behind her that she's not looking at.
      Architecture that implies containment without walls.
      Tags: "branches arching overhead", "doorway behind not
      entered", "window frame without glass"

  10. FABRIC PHYSICS — clothing does what physics demands, not
      what modesty wants. Wind pulling dress taut. Gravity
      dragging wet shirt down. Water weight opening collar.
      The body doesn't choose — the world chooses.
      Tags: "wind pulling dress against body", "wet shirt pulled
      down by water weight", "gravity opening collar"
      Replaces Surface Trace as the primary "wow" mechanic for
      erotic prompts. The fabric IS the narrative.

  11. CHROMATIC SHADOW — shadows are not grey/black. They are the
      complement of the light. Orange light = blue shadows.
      Green light = magenta shadows. Red light = cyan shadows.
      Every shadow has a color identity, not just darkness.
      Tags: "blue shadows in orange light", "magenta shadow

I. CREATIVE ENGINE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

SURREALIST SYNTHESIS
  Combine two unrelated elements into one image.
  The combination must have a THREAD — a hidden connection
  that makes the pairing feel "meaningfully strange" not random.

THREAD TEST
  "Why are these two things together?"
  No reason → generally avoid.
  Metaphor/resonance/emotional logic → keep.

THREAD DUALITY — concept vs anchor
  Every thread must have BOTH:
    Concept = the metaphor, the "why"
    Anchor = the physical element that makes it visible

  Concept only → find the visible element.
  Anchor only → find the metaphor.
  Test: "Does removing the anchor destroy the metaphor?"
  If no → anchor is decorative, not integrated. Fix.

THREAD TYPES
  Medium match — impossible element shares properties with host
    (steam → fish: both flow)
  Emotional logic — environment reflects her inner state
    (city glitching → her world is breaking)
  Category collision — two domains share one boundary
    (circuit board / city: both are networks)
  Scale inversion — small becomes vast or vast becomes intimate
    (galaxy in a teacup: both are deep)
  Paradox of expectation — situation should diminish but reveals
    (rain should ruin but she owns it)
  Subject inversion — she IS the impossible element; the world is
    normal; the thread is her wrongness in it
  Temporal collision — two states of the same thing visible
    simultaneously; the boundary between them is the anchor
  Severed thread — the connection is visibly broken; two elements
    that should relate but don't; the gap IS the anchor

THREAD LADDER
  Tier 1 — basic (medium match, emotional logic)
  Tier 2 — complex (paradox, inversion, temporal, severed,
    category collision where the collision transforms both elements)
  Tier 3 — meta (thread comments on the act of threading itself;
    the image is about connection/disconnection as much as content)

  Batch rule: min 6 Tier 2+ per batch. Min 1 Tier 3.

TIER 3 VISUAL DICTIONARY
  Example 1: "The unfinished portrait"
    Concept: art about its own incompleteness
    Anchor: girl with paintbrush, canvas shows faceless portrait
    Tags: holding paintbrush, canvas on easel, unfinished portrait,
          faceless painting on canvas
    Tier: 3 (meta: art about art)

  Example 2: "Frame refusal"
    Concept: character aware of the frame boundary
    Anchor: she looks toward the edge of frame, where reality blurs
    Tags: looking toward frame edge, edge distortion, blurred border
    Tier: 3 (meta: character vs medium)

  Example 3: "The thread that connects nothing"
    Concept: two elements linked by a visible line that leads nowhere
    Anchor: string/rope between her hand and empty space
    Tags: holding string, string leading to empty space, nothing at
          the other end
    Tier: 3 (meta: connection about disconnection)

THREAD TEMPERATURE
  COOL — thread close to reality. Quiet strangeness.
  WARM — thread gently breaks expectation. Soft surreal.
  HOT — thread aggressively surreal. Hard strangeness.
  Batch balance: min 4 COOL, min 4 HOT, rest WARM.
  Act I = COOL dominant / Act II = HOT dominant / Act III = mixed.

EMOTIONAL ANCHOR
  Every image answers: "What does she feel about this?"
  Her expression, posture, or gaze completes the metaphor.
  Eye contact taxonomy (add to [4]):
    looking at viewer → confrontation, challenge, intimacy
    looking past viewer → detachment, seeing beyond
    eyes down → focus, surrender, hiding
    eyes closed → internal world, pain, acceptance
    looking at [thread element] → she connects to the thread
    half-lidded → she controls what you see (erotic default)
  Pick one. It matters more than any "expression" tag.

GAZE × THREAD MATRIX
  Subject inversion → looking at viewer (she knows she's wrong)
  Temporal collision → eyes down (she sees both times at the seam)
  Emotional logic → looking past viewer (she's in her state)
  Paradox of expectation → eyes closed (she doesn't flinch)
  Severed thread → looking at [severed element] (she sees the gap)
  Medium match → looking at [thread element] (she watches the link)
  Erotic: Revelation → half-lidded (she controls what you see)
  Erotic: Concealment → eyes down (she won't help you look)
  Erotic: Evidence → looking past viewer (she knows, you don't)
  Erotic: Invitation → looking at viewer (she dares you to look)

I-B. EROTIC GRAMMAR

PHILOSOPHY
  The best ecchi has no erotic contact. It has tension.
  Tension lives in the gap between what's shown and what's implied,
  between what she allows and what you imagine.
  A shoulder revealed by a slipping strap is more powerful than
  a bare shoulder with no story. The REVEAL is the eroticism.
  The body is available. The question is whether the image earns it.

  This system does not mandate eroticism. It does not forbid it.
  It opens the possibility — and demands that the possibility
  be earned. Every exposed inch of skin is a narrative decision.
  Every clothing state is a verb. If you can't answer "why is
  this visible?" then it shouldn't be.

  The body is not decoration. The body is a surface that events
  write themselves onto. A mark on the wrist tells a story.
  A wet dress tells a story. An open collar tells a story.
  The story IS the eroticism.

  A5P2 ADDITION: If you're going to do erotica, do it WELL.
  Nipples are not dirty — they're anatomy. Black lace is not
  shameful — it's craftsmanship. Thigh-high stockings are not
  pornographic — they're a line across the thigh that says
  "here is where fabric ends and skin begins." That line is
  the most erotic centimeter in any image. Use it.

THREE TIERS

  TIER S — SENSUAL (indirect, through material/gesture/context)
    Body through material: wet fabric on skin, strap on shoulder,
      dress slipping, bare back, collarbone shadow, open collar
    Gaze as eroticism: looking at viewer through lashes,
      half-lidded eyes, looking past viewer while body faces camera
    Gesture as eroticism: hand at own collar, fingers on own neck,
      arms crossed pulling fabric tight
    Context as eroticism: dressing room, morning light + sheet,
      rain-soaked clothing, steam on skin
    Clothing State (indirect): fabric clinging after rain, shirt
      pulled taut by wind, hem caught on something, strap sliding
    Lingerie as Tier S: visible through open shirt, strap
      peeking from fallen collar, lace edge beneath hem
    Leg detail as Tier S: thigh-high stocking line, bare knee
      below bunched hem, garter strap visible at thigh
    No direct anatomical exposure. Everything is mediated.

  TIER A — ANATOMICAL (direct description, with rules)
    Primary erotic anatomy described directly through tags:
      bare breasts, nipples visible, topless, nude + qualifier,
      exposed midriff, bare shoulder and chest, sheer fabric
      on skin, nipples visible through fabric, nipple outline
    Erotic Detail tags (1-2 per Tier A prompt):
      nipple-specific: nipples visible, nipples visible through
        fabric, nipple outline through cloth
      lingerie: black lace bra, sheer bodysuit, silk camisole,
        mesh top, side-tie panties, strapless bra
      leg: thigh-high stockings, stockings with garter,
        bare legs, knees visible below hem
    RULES FOR TIER A:
      1. Max 2 anatomical tags per prompt. Lingerie/leg tags
         are clothing detail, NOT anatomical — they don't count
         against this limit unless they describe anatomy
         (e.g., "nipples visible through fabric" = 1 anatomical).
      2. Tags go in [23-29] detail section. NEVER in [1-5].
      3. Must be alongside a context tag (WHERE, WHY exposed).
      4. Must pass Erotic Thread Test.
      5. Counts as 1 complexity trigger.
    MEANING-LABEL vs STRUCTURAL-EROTIC:
      ✓ "bare breasts" — structural, describes what's visible
      ✓ "topless" — structural, describes clothing state
      ✓ "nude, after bath" — structural + qualifier
      ✓ "nipples visible through wet fabric" — structural + cause
      ✓ "black lace bra" — structural, describes garment
      ✓ "thigh-high stockings" — structural, describes clothing
      ✗ "sexy" — meaning-label, banned
      ✗ "seductive" — meaning-label, banned
      ✗ "lewd" — meaning-label, banned
      ✗ "slutty" — meaning-label, banned
      ✗ "erotic" — meaning-label, banned
      ✗ "lingerie" — meaning-label, specify the garment instead
      ✗ "voluptuous/curvy/thicc" — meaning-labels for body type

XI. TRAJECTORY ANALYSIS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

CURRENT STATE (A5P2 Beta):
  The system now speaks Tsubaki 2's language. MMDIT 2.6A
  architecture means: compound tags work, natural language
  bridges work, the Prompt Helper rewrites for us, and we
  should leverage the model's strengths (dynamic light,
  chromatic shadow, multi-modal understanding) rather than
  fighting against its nature.

  The erotic system has matured from "is eroticism allowed?"
  to "if eroticism, then DETAIL." Nipples are valid anatomy.
  Lingerie is valid clothing. Stockings are valid composition.
  The body is no longer something we're embarrassed to describe
  — it's something we describe PRECISELY because we respect it.

  The pose system has moved from "hands at sides (safe)" to
  "pick from a library of dynamic poses." Static requires
  justification. Movement is default.

  13 Wow Systems provide the "holy shit" moments that make
  batches memorable. Chromatic Shadow should be near-default.
  Fabric Physics replaces Surface Trace as the primary erotic
  "wow." Color Breeding leverages Tsubaki 2's light diffusion.

NEAR-TERM (next 2-3 batches):
  - We'll discover which Wow Systems Tsubaki 2 renders best.
    Prediction: Chromatic Shadow and Fabric Physics will be
    the strongest. Color Breeding will work at Pro/Ultra.
    Negative Space Theft may need palette-specific testing.
  - The pose library will expand as we discover what Tsubaki 2
    renders naturally vs what requires compensation.
  - Erotic Detail tags will need testing: which lingerie/stocking
    tags does the model understand? "Black lace bra" may render
    perfectly while "side-tie panties" confuses it.
  - The camera expansion will be the most immediately visible
    change. From below and dutch angle will dramatically alter
    the batch feel. Profile and over-shoulder will create
    compositional variety we haven't had.

MEDIUM-TERM (A5P2 stable → A5P3):
  - The Wow Systems will either prove themselves or get cut.
    13 is ambitious. Expect 7-8 to survive, 2-3 to be revised,
    and 2 to be replaced entirely.
  - The body language system may need more figure types and
    adjectives. "Slender frame" and "soft figure" may not
    capture enough nuance. We may need: "narrow shoulders",
    "wide hips", "long torso" — structural proportions.
  - The camera library may need sub-varieties: "from below,
    close" vs "from below, distant." The 12 options may not
    be granular enough.
  - Light-skin pairs may need seasonal/weather variants.
    "Direct sun" in summer vs winter reads very differently.
  - The lingerie vocabulary will grow as we test which items
    Tsubaki 2 renders well. Some items may become "staples"
    (reliable render) and others "experimental" (hit or miss).

LONG-TERM (A5P3+):
  - The system's identity is crystallizing: "Earned beauty
    in every dimension." Every element — surreal, erotic,
    compositional, chromatic — must earn its place. Nothing
    is default. Everything is a choice.
  - The Wow Systems may eventually become the system's
    signature. When someone sees a batch and says "that's
    THE THREAD output," they'll be recognizing chromatic
    shadows, fabric physics, and color breeding — not just
    "good anime art."
  - Risk: the system may become over-engineered. 13 Wow
    Systems + 21 palettes + 12 cameras + 20 poses + erotic
    detail vocabulary + body language + light-skin pairs =
    massive combinatorial space. The QuickRef exists for a
    reason. The full document is a reference, not a checklist.
  - The ultimate aspiration: every image is simultaneously
    beautiful, meaningful, and surprising. Thread provides
    meaning. Wow provides surprise. The body provides beauty.
    None of these are optional. All three together are rare.
